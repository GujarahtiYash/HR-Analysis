# HR Data Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

![HR Data Analytics Dashboard](./Screenshot%202026-01-17%20114735.png)

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project focuses on building an interactive **HR Data Analytics Dashboard** using **Power BI** to analyze employee attrition and workforce demographics.  
The dashboard helps HR teams understand key patterns in employee behavior and make data-driven decisions to improve retention and workforce planning.

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Analyze employee attrition and identify key risk factors.  
- Understand workforce distribution by age, salary, department, and tenure.  
- Provide a centralized view of HR KPIs for quick decision-making.  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies  
- **Power BI** – Dashboard creation and visualization  
- **Excel / CSV Dataset** – Data source  

---

## 📊 Key Metrics Tracked  
- Total Employees: **1470**  
- Attrition Count: **237**  
- Attrition Rate: **16.1%**  
- Average Age: **37 years**  
- Average Salary: **6.5K**  
- Average Years at Company: **7.0 years**

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- Highest attrition observed in the **26–35 age group**, indicating early-career retention challenges.  
- Majority of attrition occurs in the **“Up to 5K” salary bracket**, highlighting compensation as a major factor.  
- Departments with high attrition include:  
  - Laboratory Technician  
  - Sales Executive  
  - Research Scientist  
  - Sales Representative  
- Male attrition is higher compared to female employees.  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features  
- KPI cards for quick HR health check  
- Attrition analysis by:
  - Age  
  - Gender  
  - Salary  
  - Education  
  - Department  
  - Years at company  
- Interactive slicers for department selection  
- Clean and visually engaging UI  

---

## 🚀 Use Cases
